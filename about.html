About HVAC Tools

HVAC Tools is a simple educational website focused on practical air-conditioning and HVAC calculations.

We aim to make common calculations easier to understand for homeowners, students and HVAC technicians. Results are estimates and should be checked against equipment specifications and professional calculations where required.

Our approach

We explain inputs, assumptions and limitations rather than presenting calculator results as guaranteed engineering values.
